Cetirizine. Uses: Cetirizine is an antihistamine used to relieve allergy symptoms such as sneezing, runny nose, itchy or watery eyes, hay fever, and itchy skin rashes such as hives. It is sold under brand names such as Zyrtec.
How to take: Adults usually take one 10 mg tablet once a day, with or without food. Children's doses depend on age and must be checked on the product label or with a doctor.
Warnings: Cetirizine can cause drowsiness in some people, so be careful when driving or operating machinery until you know how it affects you. Alcohol can increase drowsiness. Ask a doctor before use if you have kidney problems, are pregnant, or are breastfeeding.
Side effects: Common side effects are drowsiness, tiredness, dry mouth, headache, and dizziness. Seek medical help if you develop swelling of the face or throat or difficulty breathing.
Interactions: Taking cetirizine with other sedating medicines, sleeping pills, or alcohol can increase sleepiness. Avoid taking it together with other antihistamines unless a doctor advises it.
